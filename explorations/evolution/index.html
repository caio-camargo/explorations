Life
Heritable traits: speed (cost ∝ speed²/engine), sense (whisker reach, cost ∝ sense), patience (energy hoarded before dividing), and engine (metabolic rate: burns more at rest, makes movement cheaper). There is no population slider — population is an outcome, not a setting.
World
There is no predator switch any more: carnivory is a gene (colour mode 7). Founders are herbivores; the dead leave carrion; whether hunting evolves is up to selection. Rings mark whoever has.
