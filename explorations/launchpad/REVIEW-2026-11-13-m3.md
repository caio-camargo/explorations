# M3 readiness, 2026-11-13 (crew and Selene: what stands between today and the finish line)
**Version**: 1.0.0 · **Author**: Caio Camargo + Claude (Fable 5.2, review 2 session) · **Created**: 2026-11-13 · **Updated**: 2026-11-13
**Status**: open. Five questions for Caio (QUEUE W56); the gaps are filed in QUEUE *Proposed*.
**Purpose**: M3 became the current milestone on 2026-11-12 (W49) and code opened with R2 (v1.340). Its finish line
(ROADMAP): *"Crew to a chosen Selene crater and home, a rover driven on real Selene ground, all from contracts."* This
walks that sentence through the design docs and the code and says, clause by clause, what is built, what is planned,
what is missing, and what Caio has to decide. At `main` = `b94dd977` (v1.341). No game code or test was edited.
**Confirmed** = a script printed it or the line was read at the place cited; **read** = from two inventories made by
research agents, spot-checked where marked. Nothing ran on the GPU or in a browser.

## 1. The short answer

**A crew can already fly to Selene, land and come home**, and a rover can be packed, deployed, driven and do science
there. Test 27 flies the crewed flight end to end; chapter 4b ends on the mission *Crew on Selene*.

**The finish line's three qualifiers are what is missing:**

## 3. A bug found on the way: a crew cannot deploy a crewed rover (confirmed)

`review/2026-11-13/crew_rover.mjs` builds a lander with the two-seat Crew capsule and the default rover folded on its
side, lands it on Selene as a fresh flight would, and asks to deploy:

```
crew: seats 2, rec.crewed true, crewOn 0, deploy refused: nobody aboard to drive it (a crewed rover needs a crewed lander)
```

`rvDeployCheck` (sim/rovers.js:178) asks `crewOn(s)`, which counts `p.crewAboard` (sim/space.js:804); that field is
set only when a vessel is loaded from the registry (space.js:785), never on a fresh flight. The default rover design
has two seats (`rvDefault`, rovers.js:34), so **the rover a player gets by default cannot be deployed on the flight
that carries it**, with or without a crew. Test 35 checks only the refusal on an uncrewed lander. An uncrewed design
(no seats) deploys.

## 4. What the finish line needs, in the order it can be built

1. **The ground goes live** (Q91: G3 to G5). Everything in the finish line that says "real" waits on it, and it is the
   largest item: a GPU session, a save migration, a tape fingerprint, test sites chosen again. Start it first.
2. **Places with names, and missions that ask for them** (new, a plan first): named craters and regions on Selene
   read from the relief, a mission or contract that names one with a radius, a landing judged by its distance from it,
   and a distance readout for a landing flown by hand. Decides "chosen" (question 1 below).
3. **The drive, judged**: a contract that asks a rover to reach a place or cover a distance on Selene, read from the
   rover's own record. Small once 2 exists.
4. **The crewed rover's bug** (§ 3). Small, and independent of the rest.
5. **Home means recovered**: the crew's return reads the recovery verdict. Small; needs question 4.
6. **The robot's `m3`**: a row that plays the missions of chapter 4b to *Crew on Selene* at a named place, deploys a
   rover and drives it, and comes home. Written last, specified now so each item above has a check to meet.
7. **EVA, if it is in** (question 2): E1 then E2, on the real ground. A second long pole beside the ground.

Crew as people (Q182, Q183, Q458) can be built at any time now; nothing in the finish line waits on them.

## 5. Five questions for Caio (QUEUE W56)

1. **Who chooses the crater?** (a) the mission names it; (b) the player picks from a few the mission offers, each with
   its own pay and difficulty (**default**: it makes the choice a decision, and the hard sites worth more); (c) the
   player picks anywhere and only the precision is judged.
2. **Is walking on Selene part of M3's finish line?** EVA.md says E2 is; ROADMAP's sentence does not. In: the crew
   leaves the lander, plants a flag, boards the rover. Out: the landing and the rover's drive finish M3 and EVA lands
   in M3's tail or M4. **Default: in, but as the last item**, so the ground and the contracts are not held up by it.
3. **The mission planner, RCS fine control, guest astronauts, crew rotation:** ROADMAP lists them under M3 and none is
   designed. **Default: out of the finish line**; the planner's Selene slice and guest astronauts get design items,
   the rest move to M3's tail.
4. **What is "home" for a crew?** **Default:** recovered by us or by a friendly power; a crew held abroad is a
   diplomatic event, not a loss, and the mission completes when they are back.
5. **Does a drive need a crew?** The finish line says "a rover driven". **Default: no**: a rover driven from home
   counts, and a crew driving one is a harder variant that pays more.
