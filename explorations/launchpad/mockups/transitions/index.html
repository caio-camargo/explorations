FENFEN SPACE AGENCY FOUNDED
A field by the sea, a budget, and a promise: "a satellite within the decade"
The site, this morning. Not much there yet.

The ministry yesterday signed the charter of a national space program, to be run from a coastal field until now used by a rocket society of forty members.

Its first director, C. Camargo, promised "something small, then something higher, then orbit." Rivals abroad are said to be further along.

The budget arrives by the quarter. Continued on page 4.
