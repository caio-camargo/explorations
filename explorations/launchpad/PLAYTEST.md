# Launchpad — playtest notes
**Version**: 0.1.6 · **Author**: Caio Camargo + Claude · **Created**: 2026-10-08 · **Updated**: 2026-10-08 · **Status**: running list
**Purpose**: Caio's observations from playing, roughly prioritised. Not a design doc: each item is a symptom plus a lead.
Coding sessions pick items from here and strike them through with the version that fixed them.

**Priority:** **P1** unblocks testing, or a bug you see on every flight · **P2** visible polish, contained fix ·
**P3** larger design/structure work, needs a plan first.
**Owner** names the session that holds that code (see `ACTIVE_WORK.md` for the worktrees).

| 14 | P3 | **Fusion propulsion for the late game** (from the news, 2026-10-08). More real techs like it in [`TECH_SCOUTING.md`](TECH_SCOUTING.md). | Real-world anchor: Pulsar Fusion's *Sunbird* confined plasma in an exhaust test in April 2026; UKAEA is doing its neutron shielding modelling; the US Defense Innovation Unit funds compact fusion propulsion and wants an orbital prototype in 2027 (company targets, not confirmed dates). Gameplay: very high Isp with modest thrust, so long continuous burns instead of impulsive ones. That needs the **low-thrust propagation** step on the roadmap (NOTES § infrastructure, item 5), and shielding mass and power make it a real trade-off. It is also the late-game answer to #12: fusion is how distant trips get short. It could arrive as an era tech driven by the world date, like the compute eras (v1.38), maybe with a development project to make it work. | economy + planning |
