# BACKLOG — ideas kept out of the queue
**Version**: 0.1.0 · **Author**: Caio Camargo + Claude · **Created**: 2026-11-11 · **Updated**: 2026-11-11
**Status**: live list (DECISIONS D31)
**Purpose**: A home for ideas that are worth keeping and are not work. It keeps the queue to what is being built, so
a stray idea costs one line here and no scope.

---

## Rules

1. **Nothing here is work.** No session builds, plans or mocks up a backlog idea. It has no Q number, no lane and no
   milestone.
2. **Only Caio promotes.** When he brings an idea back, it gets a QUEUE item (a 📝 plan first if it is large), its
   row here moves to "Promoted" with the item's number, and DECISIONS gets a line if it changes direction.
3. **One row per idea:** what the player would get, a size if someone has sized it, and a link to where it is
   written up. The detail stays in that note; this is a list.
4. **Anyone may add a row** when an idea comes up that is out of scope: Caio's stray ideas, a session's "this would
   be nice", a tier cut from a plan. Say in the reply that it went here.
5. **Not for:** bugs and playtest findings (PLAYTEST.md), follow-ups to work in progress (QUEUE § Proposed),
   questions for Caio (QUEUE § Waiting on Caio), decided designs waiting their turn (their milestone in ROADMAP).
