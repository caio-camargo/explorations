Each column is one step into a session (reloads collapsed); every session ends in the outcome column, so the three final heights compare directly — BOOKED (meeting scheduled) vs EXIT vs still browsing. Green ribbons carry bookings from the gate at the step where they happen (count printed at each departure); strict booking-event semantics — green exists only where the demo-request form is. Inclusion rule: every session shown touches at least one page of the marketing site; pure app-dashboard and docs-only sessions are excluded (count in the header). The app dashboard is an origin and an outcome, never a middle step: sessions starting in the product enter from the left, sessions that dive into it end on the right ( interior marketing→app→marketing hops are spliced out). Docs pages stay as steps — where journeys pick them up and where they lead is part of the story. *Origins are self-reported ("how did you hear about us") and known to under-attribute — treat the split as directional.
