Small self-contained experiments — simulations, toys, visual things. Built because they're interesting, not because they're needed.

Dots: friends & enemies →

Every dot picks one friend and one enemy, then chases one and flees the other while drifting toward the centre. Three rules, no physics engine, and a surprising amount of structure.

Fireflies →

Every firefly has a clock; it flashes at midnight, and seeing a flash nudges your own clock forward. Nobody is told the time — watch a field of random blinking organise itself into unison, travelling waves, or sync that keeps collapsing.

Slime mold →

Agents that deposit a scent trail and steer toward the strongest smell, three whiskers each. Nothing else — and out of it grow living transport networks: veins that form, compete, merge, and get reabsorbed. The organism that redesigned Tokyo's railways.
